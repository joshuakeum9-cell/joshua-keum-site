Featured build

DealDesk: consulting deliverables, generated

The slow part of consulting work isn't the thinking, it's producing the deliverables. DealDesk takes a company's financials and generates four of them: a business summary, an interview guide, a synergy deck, and an Excel model with a draft email. It's free, needs no login, and uses no AI in the product. Every output comes from a rules engine I wrote and tested myself.

  • Role Designed, built, and shipped it myself
  • Approach Rules engine plus browser-side document generation
  • Modeled on PwC management consulting simulation formats
  • Status Live, free, and open to anyone

    Behind the scenes

    How I built this site with Claude Code

    I don't write code. I described what I wanted in plain English and pushed back on what came back. The real work was editorial: cutting my own copy down, catching a figure in my chart that my model never verified and replacing it with a gap, and deciding what earned a place on the page. Plain HTML and CSS, no template, no page builder, hosted for free. Same instinct as DealDesk: when a process is slow and manual, get a tool to do the grinding and spend the time on judgment.

    About

    I'm a finance student at Baruch College's Zicklin School of Business (3.97 GPA, Dean's List, Zicklin Undergraduate Honors Program), and an aspiring strategy and operations consultant and business analyst.

    I grew up in a family of small business owners, which is where the interest started: I saw up close how much of running a business is repetitive work that nobody has time for. That's what I go after now. When I hit a process that's slow and manual, I build the tool that handles it, then spend the saved time on the actual thinking.

    I don't need a clean brief to do good work; I need a real problem. Across private equity, business development, and data analytics internships, plus independent work like DealDesk and my UPS memo, I've learned to do one thing well: take a messy business problem I don't own, structure it, and turn it into a recommendation someone can defend. I lean on AI to move faster without cutting corners on the analysis.

    Outside of work you'll find me on the basketball court or at the piano. I'm also into strength training, hiking, ping pong, guitar, and chess.
